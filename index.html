How Projection Works

No black box — the formula and its inputs are public.

1

Calibrate

We run our own tests directly on open-weight models we can afford — that's our Measured calibration set.

2

Correlate

Those same models have known public scores (GPQA, SWE-bench, AIME). We fit a transparent weighted formula between the two.

3

Project

Frontier models' public scores go into that formula. Out comes a Projected score, with a confidence range — never false precision.
